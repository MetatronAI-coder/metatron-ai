What We Do

Metatron AI is a structured analysis and design framework focused on defensive cybersecurity. We collect, normalize, and publish free threat intelligence from authoritative public sources so that under-resourced organizations (water utilities, rural hospitals, small municipalities, and non-profits) can see the same high-priority threats that larger organizations see.

We do not sell services. We do not run offensive operations against real systems. Everything we publish is derived from open sources and is free to use.
