## Objective

Identify high-priority visibility gaps and exploitable weaknesses at the IT/OT boundary for [Organization Name], with emphasis on systems that support critical operations.

## Scope

- **Effort:** 20 hours total  
- **Duration:** 2 weeks  
- **Deliverable:** One written report with prioritized findings and recommended next steps  

### Included

1. Review of publicly available network and asset information  
2. Assessment of remote-access and segmentation practices (based on information you provide)  
3. Mapping of known exploited vulnerabilities relevant to your environment (CISA KEV)  
4. Prioritized list of recommendations ranked by risk and effort  
5. One written report (approximately 8–12 pages)

### Out of Scope

- No active exploitation or penetration testing  
- No changes to any systems  
- No access to production OT networks without written authorization  
- No collection of personal data  

## Requirements from You

- Written authorization to proceed (one-page agreement)  
- Point of contact for questions  
- High-level description of your environment (optional but improves quality)
